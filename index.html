Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for macOS players

Is Gomoku free?
Check the installation page for current distribution and pricing (GitHub Releases are typically free; an App Store build may have a price).
Does Gomoku work offline?
Yes. Core gameplay and analysis work offline. LAN play requires a local network connection.
Is my game data uploaded anywhere?
No. Game records and logs are stored locally. LAN play transmits moves only to peers on your local network.
Which macOS versions are supported?
Gomoku supports macOS 12 Monterey and later, and is optimized for Apple Silicon (M-series).
What permissions does Gomoku need?
Gomoku may request Local Network access for LAN play and Files & Folders permission to save or open game records. Screen Recording/Accessibility are optional for auxiliary features.
